Chapter One — The Follow

It started with a follow request.

Somewhere in the endless scroll of Instagram, I found you — and stopped. I followed first, not because I had a plan, but because something about you made the scrolling feel suddenly unnecessary.

It was the way you talked, without performing for the camera. How effortlessly cute you were, in a way that felt almost unfair to the rest of my day.

I didn't know then that one small follow button would turn into this. Into us.

Chapter Two — The Hospital Parking Lot

Our first date had no candlelight. Just a car, a parking lot, and you.

You were at the hospital, looking after your maa, and somehow that became the place we chose for our first meeting. Not a café. Not a movie hall. A parking lot, tucked inside my car, borrowing an hour from everything you were carrying that day.

You kept glancing over your shoulder — scared your father might see us, scared the guards might grow suspicious. I remember how nervous you were, and how badly I wanted, more than anything, to make it feel safe instead.

Nothing happened. Nothing needed to. We just sat there and talked, and somehow it became one of the loveliest hours I've known. On another day, I picked you up and we simply drove — no destination in mind, just you, me, and the road quietly doing its job of giving us time.

Chapter Three — Puchku, Moti, and Mutton Threats

You became Puchku before you became anything else.

Somewhere along the way you stopped being just "her" and became Puchku — my Puchku. I still call you Moti sometimes, in that silly, only-us kind of way, and you still pretend to mind.
